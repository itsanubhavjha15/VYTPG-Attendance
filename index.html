Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Who this app is run by

VYTPG Attendance is built and administered by Anubhav Jha, in support of Govt. V.Y.T. P.G. Autonomous College, Durg. For any question, correction, or complaint about your data, contact Anubhav Jha directly:

  • Email: anubhavjha10@gmail.com
  • Phone: 6261874022

What data is collected

For professors and staff accounts: name, email address, phone number, department, and role (e.g. HoD, Batch Incharge).

For students: Roll Number, Name, Contact Number, Email, Section and Batch — taken from the master roster your department uploads, or added directly by your professor. Only these fields are kept; other columns from admission-office files (address, marks, parents' details, etc.) are discarded during upload and never stored.

The app also stores attendance records (date, lecture, present/absent) and notices/announcements posted by staff.

How data is used

Solely to run the attendance and notice-board system: marking and viewing attendance, letting students check their own attendance, and letting staff post/manage class and department notices. Data is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose outside the app's stated function.

How data is stored and secured

  • Accounts are authenticated through Firebase Authentication — no access without valid login credentials.
  • All traffic to and from the app is encrypted (HTTPS).
  • Data is stored in Google Cloud Firestore, encrypted at rest by Google's infrastructure.
  • Access within the app is role-scoped: students see only their own attendance, professors see only their own classes, and HoD/Course Incharge/Principal views are limited to their department or college as assigned.
  • No third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking scripts are used in this app.

Who can see your data

Your class professor(s), your department's authorized staff (Section Incharge/HoD, where applicable), the college Principal's account, and the app's administrator (for technical maintenance) can access relevant data within the scope of their role. Data is not sold or shared with outside organizations.

Legal basis for processing (Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023)

This app processes personal data in accordance with India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). Staff data is processed as part of the employment/institutional relationship with the college. Student attendance and contact data is processed for the specific, notified purpose of academic attendance tracking and college communication — a purpose you're made aware of through this notice at the time your data is collected or uploaded to the system.

Your rights as a Data Principal

Under the DPDP Act, you have the right to:

  • Obtain a summary of the personal data held about you and the processing being done with it.
  • Request correction, completion, or updating of your personal data.
  • Request erasure of your personal data once it's no longer needed for the purpose it was collected for.
  • Withdraw consent (where processing is based on consent) at any time, as easily as it was given.
  • Nominate another individual to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event of death or incapacity.
  • File a complaint if you believe your data has been mishandled.

Grievance redressal

To exercise any of the above rights, or to raise a concern about how your data is handled, contact the administrator using the details at the top of this page. Every reasonable effort will be made to respond and resolve the matter directly. If you remain unsatisfied, you have the right to file a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India, as provided under the DPDP Act.

Children's data

Some students using this app may be under 18 years of age. Where a student is a minor, their personal data is processed only for the limited purpose of academic attendance tracking, on the basis of the college's existing admission relationship with the student and their parent/guardian. The app does not use children's data for tracking, behavioral monitoring, or targeted advertising of any kind.

Data retention

Data is retained for as long as your account/class record is active in the system. If you'd like your data corrected or removed, contact the administrator using the details above.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as the app evolves. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Upload Excel / CSV file

Reads columns by name (any order) and keeps only Roll Number, Name, Mobile/Contact, Email, Section, Batch — every other column in the file (address, marks, parents' names, etc.) is ignored. Matches common header variants like ROLL_NUMBER, STUDENT_NAME, MOBILENO, E_MAIL, SECTION_NAME, and BATCH_NAME automatically. If a row has no Section/Batch, it's named from that row's Course + Semester columns instead (see below) — most admission-office exports need this. If your file has both a Section and a Batch column, every student is saved under both — a professor can then fetch by typing either the section name (e.g. "M5M6") or the batch name (e.g. "M5") as their class name. A professor can also combine several batches into one class by typing their codes together with no spaces (e.g. "B5B6B1B2" for batches B5, B6, B1 and B2) — each code just needs its own roster uploaded here first.

A student with no Section/Batch is first named from their Course + Semester columns (e.g. "M.SC. BIOTECHNOLOGY" + Sem 3 → "MSC BIOTECHNOLOGY SEM3") — that's the name a professor will type as their class name to fetch this roster. This field only kicks in for a row missing Course/Semester too; leave it blank to skip those instead.

If your file covers multiple combinations, list the codes for students who actually take this course/subject, comma-separated. Leave blank to include everyone in the file.

Sections & Batches found in this file

Each section AND each batch is saved as its own roster, so a class only ever fetches the exact section or batch it belongs to — not the whole course. Uncheck any you don't want to save right now.

One student per line: Name, Roll Number, Contact, Email (contact/email optional).
